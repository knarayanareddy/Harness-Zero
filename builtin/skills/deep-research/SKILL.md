# Deep Research Playbook

## Purpose
Use this skill when investigating technical questions, evaluating library alternatives, or conducting comprehensive background research.

## Methodology
1. **Define Objective**: Formulate primary hypothesis and search queries.
2. **Multi-Source Discovery**: Search codebase, documentation, and external references.
3. **Cross-Check Verification**: Validate facts against multiple sources.
4. **Deliver Synthesis**: Produce a structured findings report with citations and recommendations.
